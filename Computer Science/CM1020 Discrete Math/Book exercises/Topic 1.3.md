1. Use truth tables to verify these equivalences

a) p ∧ T ≡ p
b) p ∨ F ≡ p
c) p ∧ F ≡ F
d) p ∨ T ≡ T
e) p ∨ p ≡ p
f) p ∧ p ≡ p

<details>
  <summary>Solution</summary>

a) p ∧ T ≡ p

|  p  |  T  | p ∧ T |
| :-: | :-: | :---: |
|  T  |  T  |   T   |
|  F  |  T  |   F   |

its correct

b) p ∨ F ≡ p

|  p  |  F  | p ∨ F |
| :-: | :-: | :---: |
|  T  |  F  |   T   |
|  F  |  F  |   F   |

c) p ∧ F ≡ F

|  p  |  F  | p ∧ F |
| :-: | :-: | :---: |
|  T  |  F  |   F   |
|  F  |  F  |   F   |

they are equivalent

d) p ∨ T ≡ T

|  p  |  T  | p ∨ T |
| :-: | :-: | :---: |
|  T  |  T  |   T   |
|  F  |  T  |   T   |

they are equivalent

e) p ∨ p ≡ p

|  p  | p ∨ p |
| :-: | :---: |
|  T  |   T   |
|  F  |   F   |

they are equivalent

f) p ∧ p ≡ p

|  p  | p ∧ p |
| :-: | :---: |
|  T  |   T   |
|  F  |   F   |

they are equivalent

</details>
