#### Exercise 14.3

1. Convert the following decimal numbers to octal numbers:

(a) 971
(b) 2841
(c) 5014
(d) 10000
(e) 17926

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) 971</h6>

$971 \div 8 = 121$, remainder 3
$121 \div 8 = 15$, remainder 1
$15 \div 8 = 1$, remainder 7
$1 \div 8 = 0$, remainder 1

$1713_8$

$1(8^3)+7(8^2)+1(8^1)+3(8^0)=512+448+8+3=971_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 2841</h6>

$2841 \div 8 =355$, remainder 1
$355 \div 8 =44$, remainder 3
$44 \div 8 =5$, remainder 4
$5 \div 8 =0$, remainder 5

$5431_8$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) 5014</h6>

$5014\div8=626$, remainder 6
$626\div8=78$, remainder 2
$78\div8=9$, remainder 6
$9\div8=1$, remainder 1
$1\div8=0$, remainder 1

$11626_8$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) 10000</h6>

$10000\div8=1250$, remainder 0
$1250\div8=156$, remainder 2
$156\div8=19$, remainder 4
$19\div8=2$, remainder 3
$2\div8=0$, remainder 2

$23420_8$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) 17926</h6>

$17926\div8=2240$, remainder 6
$2240\div8=280$, remainder 0
$280\div8=35$, remainder 0
$35\div8=4$, remainder 3
$4\div8=0$, remainder 4

$43006_8$
