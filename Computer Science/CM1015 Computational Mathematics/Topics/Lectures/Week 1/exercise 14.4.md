1. Convert the following hexadecimal numbers to decimal numbers:

(a) 91
(b) 6C
(c) A1B
(d) F9D4
(e) ABCD

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) 91</h6>

$91_{16}=9(16^1)+1(16^0)= 144+1=145_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 6C</h6>

$6C=6(16^1)+12(16^0)=96+12=108_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) A1B</h6>

$A1B_{16}=10(16^2)+1(16^1)+11(16^0)=2560+16+11=2587_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) F9D4</h6>

$F9D4_{16}=15(16^3)+9(16^2)+13(16^1)+4(16^0)=$
$61440+2304+208+4=63.956_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) ABCD</h6>

$ABCD=10(16^3)+11(16^2)+12(16^1)+13(16^0)=$
$=40960+2816+192+13=43981_{10}$
