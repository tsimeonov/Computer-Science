1. Convert the following hexadecimal numbers to decimal numbers:

(a) 91
(b) 6C
(c) A1B

$91_{16}=9(16^1)+1(16^0)= 144+1=145_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 6C</h6>

$6C=6(16^1)+12(16^0)=96+12=108_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) A1B</h6>

$A1B_{16}=10(16^2)+1(16^1)+11(16^0)=2560+16+11=2587_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) F9D4</h6>

$F9D4_{16}=15(16^3)+9(16^2)+13(16^1)+4(16^0)=$
$61440+2304+208+4=63.956_{10}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) ABCD</h6>

$ABCD=10(16^3)+11(16^2)+12(16^1)+13(16^0)=$
$=40960+2816+192+13=43981_{10}$

2. Conver the following decimal numbers to hexademal numbers:

(a) 160
(b) 396
(c) 5010
(d) 25000
(e) 1000000

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) 160</h6>

$160\div16=10$, remainder 0
$10\div16=0$, remainder 10

$A0_{16}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 396</h6>

$396\div16=24$, remainder 12
$24\div16=1$, remainder 8
$1\div16=0$, remainder 1

$18C_{16}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) 5010</h6>

$5010 \div 16=313$, remainder 2
$313 \div 16=19$, remainder 9
$19 \div 16=1$, remainder 3
$1 \div 16=0$, remainder 1

$1392_{16}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) 25000</h6>

$1562\div16=97$, remainder 10
$97\div16=6$, remainder 1
$6\div16=0$, remainder 6

$61A8_{16}$

$3906\div16=244$, remainder 2

3. Write the following decimal numbers in binary, octal and hexadecimal form

(a) 0.25
(b) 0.75

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) 0.25</h6>

1. Binary (Base 2) - multiply by 2

$0.25 \times 2= 0.50$ -> integer part 0
$0.50 \times 2= 1.00$ -> integer part 1 (fractional part is now 0)

$0.25_{10}=0.01_2$

2. Octal (Base 8) - multiply by 8

$0.25 \times 8 = 2.00$ -> integer part 2 (fractional part is 0, stop)

$0.25_{10}=0.2å_2$

3. Hexadecimal (Base 16) multiply by 16

$0.25\times 16=4.00$ -> integer part 4 (fractioal part is now 0, stop)

$0.25_{10}=0.4_{16}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 0.75</h6>

1. Binary (Base 2) - multiply by 2

$0.75\times2=1.50$ -> integer part is 1 (fractional part is 0)
$0.50\times2=1.0$ -> integer part is 1 (fractional part is 0)

$0.75_{10}=0.11_2$

2. Octal (Base 8) - multiply by 8

$0.75\times 8=6.00$-> integer part is 6 (fractional part is 0)

$0.75_{10}=0.6_8$

3. Hexadecimal (Base 16) multiply by 16

$0.75\times16=12.00$ integer part is 12 (fractioanal part is 0)

$0.75_{10}=0.C_{16}$
