#### Working in different bases - part 2

1. Conver the base-5 number 324 to its' decimal equivalent.

Solution:

$324_5=3(5^2)+2(5^1)+4(5^0)=75+10+4=89_{10}$

---

2. Convert the base-8 number 327 to it's decimal equivalent

Solution

$327_8=3(8^2)+2(8^1)+7(8^0)=192+16+7=215_{10}$

---

Solution:

$=256+160+3=419_{16}$

4. Convert the decimal 342 to it's base 8 equivalent.

Solution:

$342 \div 8= 42$, remainder 6
$42 \div 8= 5$, remainder 2
$5 \div 8= 0$, remainder 5

$526_8$

5. Convert the decimal number 450 to it's hexadecimal equivalent.

$1 \div 16= 0$, remainder 1

$1C2_{16}$

7. Convert the binary number 1101 0111 1011 to its hexadecimal equivalent.

Solution:

$0111=0(2^3)+1(2^2)+1(2^1)+1(2^0)=0+4+2+1=7_{16}$

$1011=1(2^3)+0(2^2)+1(2^1)+1(2^0)=8+0+2+1=11_{16}$
