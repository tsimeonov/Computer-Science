#### Exercise 14.2

1. Convert the following decimal binary numbers to binary numbers

(a) 19
(b) 36
(c) 100
(d) 796
(e) 5000

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) 19</h6>

$19 \div 2= 9$, remainder 1
$9 \div 2= 4$, remainder 1
$4 \div 2= 2$, remainder 0
$2 \div 2= 1$, remainder 0
$1 \div 2= 0$, remainder 1

Binary number is: 10011

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 36</h6>

$36 \div 2= 18$, remainder 0
$18 \div 2= 9$, remainder 0
$9 \div 2= 4$, remainder 1
$4 \div 2= 2$, remainder 0
$2 \div 2= 1$, remainder 0
$1 \div 2= 0$, remainder 1

Binary number is: 100100

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) 100</h6>

$100 \div 2= 50$, remainder 0
$50 \div 2= 25$, remainder 0
$25 \div 2= 12$, remainder 1
$12 \div 2= 6$, remainder 0
$6 \div 2= 3$, remainder 0
$3 \div 2= 1$, remainder 1
$1 \div 2= 0$, remainder 1

Binary number is: 1100100

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) 796</h6>

$796 \div 2= 398$, remainder 0
$398 \div 2= 199$, remainder 0
$199 \div 2= 99$, remainder 1
$99 \div 2= 49$, remainder 1
$49 \div 2= 24$, remainder 1
$24 \div 2= 12$, remainder 0
$12 \div 2= 6$, remainder 0
$6 \div 2= 3$, remainder 0
$3 \div 2= 1$, remainder 1
$1 \div 2= 0$, remainder 1

Binary number is: 1100011100

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) 5000</h6>

$5000 \div 2= 2500$, remainder 0
$2500 \div 2= 1250$, remainder 0
$1250 \div 2= 625$, remainder 0
$625 \div 2= 312$, remainder 1
$312 \div 2= 156$, remainder 0
$156 \div 2= 78$, remainder 0
$78 \div 2= 39$, remainder 0
$39 \div 2= 19$, remainder 1
$19 \div 2= 9$, remainder 1
$9 \div 2= 4$, remainder 1
$4 \div 2= 2$, remainder 0
$2 \div 2= 1$, remainder 0
$1 \div 2= 0$, remainder 1

Binary number is: 1001110001000
