#### pp.131–132, Self-assessment questions 12.3 and question 3 of Exercise 12.3

1. Write down the first four terms of the geometric sequence with first term 8 and common ration 3

Solution:

- first term: 8
- second term: $8\times3=24$
- third term: $8\times3^2=8\times9=72$
- forth term: $8\times3^3=8\times27=216$

---

2. Write down the first four terms of the geometric progression with first term $\frac{1}{4}$ and common ratio $\frac{4}{5}$

Solution:

- first term: $\frac{1}{4}=0.25$
- second term: $\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{4}{5}=\frac{4}{20}=\frac{1}{5}$
- third term: $\frac{1}{4} \times (\frac{4}{5})^2=\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{16}{25}=\frac{16}{100}=\frac{4}{5}$
- forth term: $\frac{1}{4} \times (\frac{4}{5})^3=\frac{1}{4} \times \frac{64}{125}=\frac{1}{1} \times \frac{16}{125}=\frac{16}{125}$

---

3. A geometric sequence has first term 4 and common ratio 2. Find
   (a) the 5th term
   (b) the 11th term

Solution:

(a) the 5th term: $4\times2^4=4\times16=64$
(b) the 11th term: $4\times2^{10}=4\times1024=4096$
