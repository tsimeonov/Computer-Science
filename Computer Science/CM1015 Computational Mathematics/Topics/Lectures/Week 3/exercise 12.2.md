#### p.129, Self-assessment questions 12.2 and questions 3 and 4 of Exercise 12.2

1. Write down the first five terms of the arithmetic progression with common difference 4 and first term 1.

Solution

- term 1: 1
- term 2: 1 + 4 = 5
- term 3: 1 + 2(4) = 1 + 8 = 9
- term 4: 1 + 3(4) = 1 + 12 = 13
- term 5: 1 + 4(4) = 1 + 16 = 17

---

2. Write down the first six terms of the arithmetic progression with common difference -1 and first term 3

Solution:

- term 1: 3
- term 2: 3 + (-1) = 2
- term 3: 3 + 2(-1) = 3 + (-2) = 1
- term 4: 3 + 3(-1) = 3 + (-3) = 0
- term 5: 3 + 4(-1) = 3 + (-4) = -1
- term 6: 3 + 5(-1) = 3 + (-5) = -2
