#### Non-integer numbers conversion

1. In the decimal syste, what does the number 37.3 represents?

- $3\times10+7\times1+0.3\times10^0$
- $3\times10^0+7\times10^0+3\times10^1$
- $3\times10^1+7\times10^1+3\times10^{-1}$
- $3\times10^1+7\times10^0+3\times10^{-1}$

Solution:

- $3\times10^1+7\times10^0+3\times10^{-1}$
