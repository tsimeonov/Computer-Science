### Operations with binary numbers

1. Subtract the binary numbers 1010 from 1101

$1010_2=1(2^3)+0(2^2)+1(2^1)+0(2^0)=$
$8+2=10_{10}$

$1101_2=1(2^3)+1(2^2)+0(2^1)+1(2^0)=$
$8+4+1=13_{10}$

$3 \div 2 = 1$, remainder 1
$1 \div 2 = 0$, remainder 1

$3_{10}=0011_2$

---

2. Subtract the binary number 1001 from 1100.

$1001_2=1(2^3)+0(2^2)+0(2^1)+1(2^0)=$
$8+1=9_{10}$

$1100_2=1(2^3)+1(2^2)+0(2^1)+0(2^0)=$
$8+4=12_{10}$

$12-9=3_{10}$

$3 \div 2 = 1$, remainder 1
$1 \div 2 = 0$, remainder 1

$3_{10}=0011_2$

---

3. Subtract the binary number 10101 from 11011

$10101_2=1(2^4)+0(2^3)+1(2^2)+0(2^1)+1(2^0)=$
$16+4+1=21_{10}$

$11011_2=1(2^4)+1(2^3)+0(2^2)+1(2^1)+1(2^0)=$
$16+8+2+1=27_{10}$

$27-21=6_{10}$

$6 \div 2= 3$ remainder 0
$3 \div 2= 1$ remainder 1
$1 \div 2= 0$ remainder 1

$6_{10}=0110_2$

---

4. Multiply the binary numbers 10111 and 101

$
\begin{array}{r}
  10111 \\
  \times\; 101 \\
  \hline
  10111 \\
  00000\phantom{0} \\
  +\; 10111\phantom{00} \\
  \hline
  1110011
\end{array}
$

---

5. Multiply the binary numbers 110 and 101

$

  \times\; 101 \\
  \hline
  110 \\
  000\phantom{0} \\
  +\; 110\phantom{00} \\
  \hline
  11110
\end{array}
$

---

6. Multiply the binary numbers 111 and 11

$111 = 1(2^2)+1(2^1)+1(2^0)=$
$4+2+1=7$

$11 = 1(2^1)+1(2^0)=$
$2+1=3$

$21 \div 2= 10$ remainder 1
$10 \div 2= 5$ remainder 0
$5 \div 2= 2$ remainder 1
$2 \div 2= 1$ remainder 0
$1 \div 2= 0$ remainder 1

$21_{10}=10101_2$
