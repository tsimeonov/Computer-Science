##### 1. E = {all positive integers}, X={all positive integers}, Y={10,20,50}, Z={5,10,15,20}. State whether the following are true or false.

(a) Y ⊆ X

(b) 10 ∈ X

(c) 15 $\notin$ X

(d) Y ⊆ Z

(e) Z ⊆ X

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) Y ⊆ X</h6>

True

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) 10 ∈ X</h6>

True

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) 15 $\notin$ X</h6>

False

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) Y ⊆ Z</h6>

False

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) Z ⊆ X</h6>

True
