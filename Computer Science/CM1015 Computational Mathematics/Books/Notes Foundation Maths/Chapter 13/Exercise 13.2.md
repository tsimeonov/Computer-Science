##### 1. List the elements of the following finite sets:

A = {x: x is a positive integer, greater than 5 and less than 11}
B = {x: x is odd and x is greater than 5 and less than 20}
C = {x: x is between 10 and 50 inclusive and x is divisible by 3 and x is divisible by 5}

Solution

A = {6,7,8,9,10}
B = {7,9,11,13,15,17,19}
C = {15,30,45}
