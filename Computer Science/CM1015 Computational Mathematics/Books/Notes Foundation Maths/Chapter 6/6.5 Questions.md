#### 1. Express each of the following numbers in scientific notation

- 45
- 45000
- -450
- 90000000
- 0.15
- 0.00036
- 3.5
- -13.2
- 1000000
- 0.0975
- 45.34

Solution

- 45 = $4.5 \times 10^1$
  </br>

- 45000 = $45 \times 10^3 $
  </br>

- -450 = $-4.5 \times 10^2$
  </br>

- 90000000 = $9 \times 10^7$
  </br>

- 0.15 = $1.5 \times 10^{-1}$
  </br>

- 0.00036 = $36 \times 10^{-4}$
  </br>

- 3.5 = $35 \times 10^(-2)$
  </br>

- -13.2 = $-1.32 \times 10^{1}$
  </br>

- 1000000 = $1 \times 10{^6}$
  </br>

- 0.0975 = $9.75 \times 10{-2}$
  </br>

- 45.34 = $4.534 \times 10{1}$

#### 3. Simplify each of the following expressions

- $(4 \times 10^3) \times (6 \times 10^4)$
- $(9.6 \times 10^4) \times (8.3 \times 10^3)$
- $(1.2 \times 10^{-3}) \times (8.7 \times 10^{-2})$
- $\frac{9.35 \times 10^{4}}{6.14 \times 10^{5}}$
- $\frac{4.96 \times 10^{-2}}{9.37 \times 10^{-5}}$

Solution

- $(4 \times 10^3) \times (6 \times 10^4)$
  $24 \times 10^7$
  $2.4 \times 10^8$
  <br>

- $(9.6 \times 10^4) \times (8.3 \times 10^3)$
  $(9.6 \times 8.3) \times (10^4 \times 10^3)$
  $(79.68) \times (10^7)$
  $(7.968) \times (10^8)$
  <br>

- $(1.2 \times 10^{-3}) \times (8.7 \times 10^{-2})$
  $(1.2 \times 8.7)(10^{-3}\times 10^{-2})$
  $(10.44)(10^{-3+(-2)})$
  $(10.44)(10^{-5})$
  $(1.044)(10^{-4})$
  <br>

- $\frac{9.35 \times 10^{4}}{6.14 \times 10^{5}}$
  $(\frac{9.35}{6.14}) \times (\frac{10^4}{10^5})$
  $1.52 \times 10^{-1}$
  <br>

- $\frac{4.96 \times 10^{-2}}{9.37 \times 10^{-5}}$
  $(\frac{4.96}{9.37}) \times (\frac{10^{-2}}{10^{-5}})$
  $0,529 \times 10^{-2-(-5)}$
  $0,529 \times 10^{-2+5}$
  $0,529 \times 10^{3}$
  $5.29 \times 10^{2}$
