#### 1. Express each of the following numbers in scientific notation

- 45
- 45000
- -450
- 90000000
- 0.15
- 0.00036
- 3.5
- -13.2
- 1000000
- 0.0975
- 45.34

Solution

- 45 = $4.5 \times 10^1$
  </br>

- 45000 = $45 \times 10^3 $
  </br>

- -450 = $-4.5 \times 10^2$
  </br>

- 90000000 = $9 \times 10^7$
  </br>

- 0.15 = $1.5 \times 10^{-1}$
  </br>

- 0.00036 = $36 \times 10^{-4}$
  </br>

- 3.5 = $35 \times 10^(-2)$
  </br>

- -13.2 = $-1.32 \times 10^{1}$
  </br>

- 1000000 = $1 \times 10{^6}$
  </br>

- 0.0975 = $9.75 \times 10{-2}$
  </br>

- 45.34 = $4.534 \times 10{1}$
