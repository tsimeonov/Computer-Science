#### 6.1 The laws of indices

- First law
  $a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$

- Second law
  $\frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$

- Key point: any number raised to the power 0 equals 1, that is $a^0 = 1$

- Third law
  $(a^m)^n = a^{mn}$

- Key point: $(a^mb^n)^k = a^{mk}b^{nk}$

#### 6.2 Negative powers

- key poits - $a^{-m} = \frac{1}{a^m}$, $a^{m} = \frac{1}{a^{-m}}$
