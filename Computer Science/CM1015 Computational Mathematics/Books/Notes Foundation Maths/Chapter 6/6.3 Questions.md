###### 1. Evaluate

- $64^{1/3}$
- $144^{1/2}$
- $16^{-1/4}$
- $25^{-1/2}$
- $\frac{1}{32^{-1/5}}$

Solution

- $64^{1/3}$
  $\sqrt[3]{64}$
  $4\times 4\times 4 = 64$
  </br>
- $144^{1/2}$
  $\sqrt{144}$
  $12 \times 12 = 144 $
  </br>
- $16^{-1/4}$
  $\frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{16}}$
  $ 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 16$
  $ \frac{1}{2}$
  </br>
- $25^{-1/2}$
  $\frac{1}{25^{\frac{1}{2}}}$
  $\frac{1}{\sqrt{25}}$
  $\frac{1}{5}$
  </br>
- $\frac{1}{32^{-1/5}}$
  $32^{1/5}$
  $\sqrt[5]{32} = 2$
