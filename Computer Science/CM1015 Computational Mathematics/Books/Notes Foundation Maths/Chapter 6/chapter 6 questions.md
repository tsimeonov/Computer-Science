#### 1. Simplify the following expressions

- $\sqrt(\frac{27}{y^3})^{-\frac{2}{3}}$
- $(\frac{48x^7y^{-1}}{3x^{-1}y^{-3}})^{-\frac{3}{4}}$
- $\sqrt {\sqrt[a]{\frac{b}{a}} \times\frac{c^2\sqrt{ab}}{b^{-1}c^{-2}}}$

Solution

- $\sqrt(\frac{27}{y^3})^{-\frac{2}{3}}$
  Step 1: Convert the square root to a fractional exponent
  $((\frac{27}{y^3})^{\frac{1}{2}})^{-\frac{2}{3}}$
  Step 2: Multiply the nested exponent
  $(\frac{27}{y^3})^{-\frac{1}{3}}$
  Step 3: Handle the negative exponent
  $(\frac{y^3}{27})^{\frac{1}{3}}$
  Step 4: Apply the remaining exponent

  - Numerator: $(y^3)^{\frac{1}{3}} = y$
  - Demominator: $(27)^{\frac{1}{3}} = 3$

  This leaves us with: $\frac{y}{3}$
  <br>

- $(\frac{48x^7y^{-1}}{3x^{-1}y^{-3}})^{-\frac{3}{4}}$
  Step 1: Simplify the coefficients
  $\frac{48}{3} = 16$
  $(\frac{16x^7y^{-1}}{x^{-1}y^{-3}})^{-\frac{3}{4}}$
  Step 2: Simplify the variables using ecponent rules

  - For x: $7 - (-1) = 7 + 1 = 8 => x^8$
  - For y: $-1-(-3) = -1+3 = 2 => y^2$

  The entire fraction is now simplified to a single line without a denominator

  $(16x^8y^2)^{-\frac{3}{4}}$

  Step 3: Distribute the outer exponent
  Apply the $-\frac{3}{4}$ power to each individual component inside the parentheses:

  $16^{-\frac{3}{4}} \times (x^8)^{-\frac{3}{4}} \times (y^2)^{-\frac{3}{4}}$

  Step 4: Calculate each component

  - $16^{-\frac{3}{4}} = 2^{4 \times -\frac{3}{4}} = 2^{-3} = \frac{1}{2^3} =\frac{1}{8}$
  - $(x^8)^{-\frac{3}{4}} = 8 \times -\frac{3}{4} = -6 => x^{-6}$
  - $(y^2)^{-\frac{3}{4}} = 2 \times -\frac{3}{4} = -\frac{3}{2} = y^{-\frac{3}{2}}$

  Combine these gives

  $\frac{1}{8}x^{-6}y^{-\frac{3}{2}}$

  Step 5: Convert the positive exponents

  $\frac{1}{8x^6y^{\frac{3}{2}}}$

 <br>

- $\sqrt {\sqrt[a]{\frac{b}{a}} \times\frac{c^2\sqrt{ab}}{b^{-1}c^{-2}}}$

Step 1: Convert inner radicals to fractional exponents
$\sqrt{(\frac{b}{a})^{\frac{1}{a}} \times \frac{c^2(ab)^{\frac{1}{2}}}{b^{-1}c^{-2}}}$

Step 2: Simplify the right-hand fraction

- $(ab)^{\frac{1}{2}} = a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{\frac{1}{2}}$
- $b^{-1}c^{-2} = c^{2}a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{1}c^{2}$
  $a{\frac{1}{2} \times (b{^{\frac{1}{2}}}\times b^{1}) \times (c{^{2}}\times c^{2})}$
  $b^{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2}{2}} = b^{\frac{3}{2}}$
  $c^{2+2} = c^4$
  $a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{\frac{3}{2}}c^4$

Step 3: Simplify the left-hand fraction
$(\frac{b}{a}^{\frac{1}{a}})$
$b^{\frac{1}{a}}a^{-\frac{1}{a}}$

Step 4: Combine all terms inside the outer square root
$\sqrt{b^{\frac{1}{a}}a^{-\frac{1}{a}} \times  a^{\frac{1}{2}}b^{\frac{3}{2}}c^4}$

Combine the matching variables by adding their exponents:

- For a: $-\frac{1}{a}+\frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{a}$
- For b: $\frac{1}{a}+\frac{3}{2} = \frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{a}$
- For c: 4

This gives the fully combined inner equation:
$\sqrt{a^{\frac{1}{2}-\frac{1}{a}}b^{\frac{3}{2}+\frac{1}{a}}c^4}$

Step 5: Distribute the outer square root
Raise the entire expression to the $\frac{1}{2}$ power by multiplying every
exponent by $\frac{1}{2}$

- For a: $(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{a}) \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{2a}$
- For b: $(\frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{a}) \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{2a}$
- For c: $4 \times \frac{1}{2} = 2$

Final answer:

$a^{\frac{1}{4}-\frac{1}{2a}}b^{\frac{3}{4}+\frac{1}{2a}}c^2$
