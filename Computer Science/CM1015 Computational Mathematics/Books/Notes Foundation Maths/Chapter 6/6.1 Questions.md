1. Simplify

- $5^7 \times 5^{13}$
- $9^8 \times 9^5$
- $11^2 \times 11^3 \times 11^4$

Solution

- $5^7 \times 5^{13} = 5^{20}$
- $9^8 \times 9^5 = 9^{13}$
- $11^2 \times 11^3 \times 11^4 = 11^{9}$

---

2. Simplify

- $\frac{15^3}{15^2}$
- $\frac{4^{18}}{4^9}$
- $\frac{5^{20}}{5^{19}}$

Solution

- $\frac{15^3}{15^2} = 15^{3-2} = 15$
- $\frac{4^{18}}{4^9} = 4^{18-9} = 4^9 $
- $\frac{5^{20}}{5^{19}} = 5$

---

3. Simplify:

- $a^7a^3$
- $a^4a^5$
- $b^{11}b^{10}b$

Solution

- $a^7a^3 = a^{10}$
- $a^4a^5 = a^9$
- $b^{11}b^{10}b = b^{22}$

6. Simplify

- $(7^3)^2$
- $(4^2)^8$
- $(7^9)^2$

Solution

- $(7^3)^2 = 7^{3\times2} = 7^6$
- $(4^2)^8 = 3^{16}$
- $(7^9)^2 = 7^{18}$
