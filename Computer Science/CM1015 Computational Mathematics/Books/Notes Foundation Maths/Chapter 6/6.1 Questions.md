1. Simplify

- $5^7 \times 5^{13}$
- $9^8 \times 9^5$
- $11^2 \times 11^3 \times 11^4$

Solution

- $5^7 \times 5^{13} = 5^{20}$
- $9^8 \times 9^5 = 9^{13}$
- $11^2 \times 11^3 \times 11^4 = 11^{9}$

2. Simplify

- $\frac{15^3}{15^2}$
- $\frac{4^{18}}{4^9}$
- $\frac{5^{20}}{5^{19}}$

Solution

- $\frac{15^3}{15^2} = 15^{3-2} = 15$
- $\frac{4^{18}}{4^9} = 4^{18-9} = 4^9 $
- $\frac{5^{20}}{5^{19}} = 5$

---

3. Simplify:

- $a^7a^3$

- $b^{11}b^{10}b$

Solution

- $a^4a^5 = a^9$
- $b^{11}b^{10}b = b^{22}$

6. Simplify

10. Simplify

- $\frac{(z^2)^3}{z^3}$
- $\frac{(y^3)^2}{(y^2)^2}$
- $\frac{(x^3)^2}{(x^2)^3}$

Solution

- $\frac{(z^2)^3}{z^3} = \frac{z^6}{z^3} = z^3$
- $\frac{(y^3)^2}{(y^2)^2}= \frac{y^6}{y^4} = y^2$
- $\frac{(x^3)^2}{(x^2)^3} = \frac{x^6}{x^6} = x^0 = 1$
