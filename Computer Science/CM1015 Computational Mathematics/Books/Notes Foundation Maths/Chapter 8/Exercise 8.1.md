#### 8.1 Exercise

##### 1. Remove the brackets from

- $9(x+3)$
- $-5(x-2)$
- $\frac{1}{2}(x+1)$
- $-(a-3b)$
- $\frac{1}{2(x+y)}$
- $\frac{x}{y(x-y)}$

Solution

- $9(x+3)=9x+27$
  <br>
- $-5(x-2)=-5x+10$
  <br>

- $\frac{1}{2}(x+1)=\frac{1}{2}x+\frac{1}{2}$
  <br>

- $-(a-3b)=-a+3b$
  <br>

- $\frac{1}{2(x+y)}=\frac{1}{2x+2y}$
  <br>

- $\frac{x}{y(x-y)}=\frac{x}{yx-y^2}$
