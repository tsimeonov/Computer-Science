##### 1. Remove the brackets from

- $9(x+3)$
- $-5(x-2)$
- $\frac{1}{2}(x+1)$
- $-(a-3b)$
- $\frac{1}{2(x+y)}$
- $\frac{x}{y(x-y)}$

Solution

- $9(x+3)=9x+27$
  <br>
- $-5(x-2)=-5x+10$
  <br>

- $\frac{1}{2}(x+1)=\frac{1}{2}x+\frac{1}{2}$
  <br>

- $-(a-3b)=-a+3b$
  <br>

- $\frac{1}{2(x+y)}=\frac{1}{2x+2y}$
  <br>

- $\frac{x}{y(x-y)}=\frac{x}{yx-y^2}$

##### 3. Factorise

- $3x+18=$
- $3y-9=$
- $-3y-9=$
- $-3-9y=$
- $20+5t=$
- $20-5t=$
- $-5t-20=$
- $3x+12=$
- $17t+34=$
- $-36+4t=$

Solution

- $3x+18=3(x+6)$
  <br>
- $3y-9=3(y-3)$
  <br>

- $-3y-9=-3(y+3)$
  <br>

- $-3-9y=-3(1+3y)$
  <br>

- $20+5t=5(4+t)$
  <br>

- $20-5t=5(4-t)$
  <br>

- $-5t-20=-5(t+4)$
  <br>

- $3x+12=3(x+4)$
  <br>

- $17t+34=17(t+2)$
  <br>

- $-36+4t=-4(9-t)$

##### 4. Factorise

- (a) $x^4+2x$
- (b) $x^4-2x$
- (c) $3x^4-2x$
- (d) $3x^4+2x$
- (e) $3x^4+2x^2$
- (f) $3x^2+2x^3$
- (g) $17z-z^2$
- (h) $-xy+3x$
- (i) $-xy+3y$
- (j) $x+2xy+3xyz$

Solution

- (a) $x^4+2x = x(x^3+2)$
  <br>
- (b) $x^4-2x=x(x^3-2)$
  <br>

- (c) $3x^4-2x=x(3x^3-2)$
  <br>

- (d) $3x^4+2x=x(3x^3+2)$
  <br>

- (e) $3x^4+2x^2=x^2(3x^2+2)$
  <br>

- (f) $3x^2+2x^3=x^2(3+2x)$
  <br>

- (g) $17z-z^2=z(17-z)$
  <br>

- (h) $-xy+3x=x(-y+3)$
  <br>

- (i) $-xy+3y=y(-x+3)$
  <br>

- (j) $x+2xy+3xyz=x(1+2y+3yz)$
