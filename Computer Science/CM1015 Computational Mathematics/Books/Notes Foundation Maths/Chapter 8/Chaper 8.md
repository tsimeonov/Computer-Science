##### 1. Factorise the following expressions

- (a) $7x+49$
- (b) $121x+22y$
- (c) $a^2+ab$
- (d) $ab+b^2$
- (e) $ab^2+ba^2$

Solution

- (a) $7x+49= 7(x+7)$
  <br>
- (b) $121x+22y=11(11x+2y)$
  <br>

- (c) $a^2+ab=a(a+b)$
  <br>

- (d) $ab+b^2=b(a+b)$
  <br>

- (e) $ab^2+ba^2=ab(b+a)=ab \times b+ab \times a=ab^2+a^2b= ab^2+ba^2 $

##### 2. Factorise the following expressions

- (a) $3x^2+x-2$
- (b) $x^2-144$
- (c) $s^2-5s+6$
- (d) $2y^2-y-15$

Solution

(a) $3x^2+x-2$
<b>Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers</b>
$3 \times (-2)=-6$

<b>Step 2: Find the magic numbers</b>

1. Multiply yo give -6:
2. Add to give 1:

The numbers are 3 and -2

<b>Step 3: Split the middle term</b>
$3x^2+ 3x-2x-2$

<b>Step 4: Factor the grouping</b>

1. Left side: $3x^2+3x=3x(x+1)$
2. Right side: $-(2x-2)=-2(x+1)$

<b>Step 5: Combine into the final answer</b>
$(x+1)(3x-2)=3x^2-2x+3x-2= 3x^2+x-2$

<br>

(b) $x^2-144= (x-12)(x+12)=x^2+12x-12x-144=x^2-144$
<br>
(c) $s^2-5s+6$

<b>Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers<b>
$1 \times 6 =6$

<b>Step 2: Find the magic numbers<b>

1. Multiply to give 6: $(-2)\times(-3)= 6$
2. Add to give -5: $(-2)+(-3)=-5$

The numbers -2 and -3

<b>Step 3: Split the middle term<b>
$s^2-2s-3s+6$

<b>Step 4: Multiply the first and last numbers<b>

1. Left side: $s^2-2s=s(s-2)$
2. Right side: $-3s+6=-3(s-2)$

<b>Step 5: Multiply the first and last numbers<b>
$(s-2)(s-3)=s^2-3s-2s+6=s^2-5s+6$

(d) $2y^2-y-15$

<b>Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers</b>
$2 \times -15=-30$

<b>Step 2: Find the magic numbers</b>

1. Multiply to give -30:
2. Add to give -1

The numbers are 5 and -6

$2y^2-6y+5y-15$

2. Right side: $5y-15=5(y-3)$

<b>Step 5: Combine the final answer</b>
$(y-3)(2y+5)=2y^2+5y-6y-15=2y^2-y-15$

##### 3. Factorise the following expressions

- (a) $1-x^2$
- (b) $x^2-1$
- (c) $9-x^2$
- (d) $x^2-81$
- (e) $25-y^2$

Solution

(a) $1-x^2=(1-x)(1+x)=1+1x-1x-x^2=1-x^2$

(b) $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)=x^2+1x-1x-1=x^2-1$

(c) $9-x^2=(3-x)(3+x)=9+3x-3x-x^2=9-x^2$

(d) $x^2-81=(x-9)(x+9)=x^2+9x-9x-81=x^2-81$

(e) $25-y^2=(5-y)(5+y)=25+5y-5y-y^2=25-y^2$
