##### 1. Factorise the following expressions

- (a) $7x+49$
- (b) $121x+22y$
- (c) $a^2+ab$
- (d) $ab+b^2$
- (e) $ab^2+ba^2$

Solution

- (a) $7x+49= 7(x+7)$
  <br>
- (b) $121x+22y=11(11x+2y)$
  <br>

- (c) $a^2+ab=a(a+b)$
  <br>

- (d) $ab+b^2=b(a+b)$
  <br>

- (e) $ab^2+ba^2=ab(b+a)=ab \times b+ab \times a=ab^2+a^2b= ab^2+ba^2 $

##### 3. Factorise the following expressions

- (a) $1-x^2$
- (b) $x^2-1$
- (c) $9-x^2$
- (d) $x^2-81$
- (e) $25-y^2$

Solution

(a) $1-x^2=(1-x)(1+x)=1+1x-1x-x^2=1-x^2$

(b) $x^2-1=(x-1)(x+1)=x^2+1x-1x-1=x^2-1$

(c) $9-x^2=(3-x)(3+x)=9+3x-3x-x^2=9-x^2$

(d) $x^2-81=(x-9)(x+9)=x^2+9x-9x-81=x^2-81$

(e) $25-y^2=(5-y)(5+y)=25+5y-5y-y^2=25-y^2$

##### 4. Factorise the denominators of the following expressions:

(a) $\frac{1}{x^2+6x}$

(b) $\frac{3}{s^2+3s+2}$

(c) $\frac{3}{s^2+s-2}$

(d) $\frac{5}{x^2+11x+28}$

(e) $\frac{x}{2x^2-17x-9}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{x^2+6x}=\frac{1}{x(x+6)}$

(b) $\frac{3}{s^2+3s+2}$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers
$1 \times 2 =2 $

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply to give: 2
2. Add to give: 3

The numbers are 1 and 2

Step 3: Split the middle term
$s^2+1s+2s+2$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $s^2+1s=s(s+1)$
2. Right side: $2s+2=2(s+1)$

Step 5: Combine the final answer
$(s+1)(s+2)=s^2+2s+1s+2=s^2+3s+2$

Final answer:

$\frac{1}{(s+1)(s+2)}$

(c) $\frac{3}{s^2+s-2}$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers
$1\times -2=-2$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply to give 2: $1\times2=2$
2. Add to give -1: $-1+2=1$

The numbers are -1 and 2

Step 3: Split the middle term

$s^2-1s+2s-2$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $s^2-1s=s(s-1)$
2. Right side: $(2s-2)=2(s-1)$

Step 5: Combine the final answer
$(s-1)(s+2)=s^2+2s-1s-2s=s^2+s-2$

Final answer

$\frac{1}{(s-1)(s+2)}$

(d) $\frac{5}{x^2+11x+28}$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers
$1\times 28=28$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply to give: 28
2. Add to give: 11

The nuumbers are 4 and 7

Step 3: Split the middle term
$x^2+4x+7x+28$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $(x^2+4x)=x(x+4)$
2. Right side: $7x+28=7(x+4)$

Step 5: Combine the final answer
$(x+4)(x+7)=x^2+7x+4x+28=x^2+11x+28$

Final answer
$\frac{5}{(x+4)(x+7)}$

(e) $\frac{x}{2x^2-17x-9}$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers
$2\times (-9)=-18$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply to give -18: $1\times-18=-18$
2. Add to give -17: $1 + (-18)=-17$

The numbers are 1 and -18

Step 3: Split the middle term
$2x^2+1x-18x-9$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $2x^2+1x=x(2x+1)$
2. Right side: $-18x-9=-9(2x+1)$

Step 5: Combine the final answer
$(2x+1)(x-9)=2x^2-18x+1x-9=2x^2-17x-9$

Final answer

$\frac{x}{(2x+1)(x-9)}$
