#### 8.3 Exercise

##### 1. Factories the following quadratic expressions

- (a) $x^2+3x+2$
- (b) $x^2+13x+42$
- (c) $x^2+2x-15$
- (d) $x^2+9x-10$
- (e) $x^2-11x+24$
- (f) $x^2-100$
- (g) $x^2+4x+4$
- (h) $x^2-36$
- (i) $x^2-25$
- (j) $x^2+10x+9$
- (k) $x^2+8x-9$
- (l) $x^2-8x-9$
- (m) $x^2-10x+9$
- (n) $x^2-5x$

Solution

- (a) $x^2+3x+2=(x+1)(x+2)$
  <br>
- (b) $x^2+13x+42=(x+6)(x+7)$
  <br>

- (c) $x^2+2x-15=(x-3)(x+5)$
  <br>

- (d) $x^2+9x-10=(x+10)(x-1)$
  <br>

- (e) $x^2-11x+24$
  <br>

- (f) $x^2-100$
  <br>

- (g) $x^2+4x+4$
  <br>

- (h) $x^2-36$
  <br>

- (i) $x^2-25$
  <br>

- (j) $x^2+10x+9$
  <br>

- (k) $x^2+8x-9$
  <br>

- (l) $x^2-8x-9$
  <br>

- (m) $x^2-10x+9$
  <br>

- (n) $x^2-5x$
