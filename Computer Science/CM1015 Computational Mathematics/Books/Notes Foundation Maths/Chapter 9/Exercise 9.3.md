#### 9.3 Exercise

##### 1. Simplify

(a) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{y}{3}$

(b) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{z}{2}$

(c) $\frac{2}{5}of\frac{1}{y}$

(d) $\frac{2}{5}of\frac{1}{x}$

(e) $\frac{3}{4}of\frac{x}{y}$

(f) $\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{x^2}{y}$

(g) $\frac{x}{y}\times\frac{3}{5}$

(h) $\frac{7}{8}\times\frac{x}{2y}$

(i) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{1}{2x}$

(j) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{x}{2}$

(k) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{2}{x}$

(l) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{x}{3}$

(m) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{3}{x}$

(n) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{1}{3x}$

(o) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{3x}{2}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{y}{3}=\frac{1y}{6}$

(b) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{z}{2}=\frac{1z}{6}$

(c) $\frac{2}{5}of\frac{1}{y}=\frac{2}{5y}$

(d) $\frac{2}{5}of\frac{1}{x}=\frac{2}{5x}$

(e) $\frac{3}{4}of\frac{x}{y}=\frac{3x}{4y}$

(f) $\frac{3}{5}\times\frac{x^2}{y}=\frac{3x^2}{5y}$

(g) $\frac{x}{y}\times\frac{3}{5}=\frac{3x}{5y}$

(h) $\frac{7}{8}\times\frac{x}{2y}=\frac{7x}{16y}$

(i) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{1}{2x}=\frac{1}{4x}$

(j) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{x}{2}=\frac{1x}{4}$

(k) $\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{2}{x}=\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{1}{x}$

(l) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{x}{3}=\frac{x}{9}$

(m) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{3}{x}=\frac{3}{3x}=\frac{1}{x}$

(n) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{1}{3x}=\frac{1}{9x}$

(o) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{3x}{2}=\frac{3x}{6}=\frac{x}{2}$

##### 2. Simplify

(a) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{x}{2}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{2}{x}$

(c) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{2}{x}$

(d) $\frac{x}{2}\div\frac{1}{2}$

(e) $\frac{2}{x}\div 2$

(f) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{x}{2}=\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{2}{x}=\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{1}{x}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{2}{x}=\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{x}{2}=\frac{x}{4}$

(c) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{2}{x}=\frac{2}{x}\times\frac{x}{2}=\frac{2x}{2x}=1$

(d) $\frac{x}{2}\div\frac{1}{2}=\frac{x}{2}\times\frac{2}{1}=\frac{2x}{2}=x$

(e) $\frac{2}{x}\div 2=\frac{2}{x}\times\frac{1}{2}=\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{1}{x}$

(f) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}=\frac{2}{x}\times\frac{2}{1}=\frac{4}{x}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}=\frac{3}{x}\times\frac{2}{1}=\frac{6}{x}$
