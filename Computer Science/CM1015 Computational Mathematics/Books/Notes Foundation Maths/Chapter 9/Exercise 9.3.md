##### 2. Simplify

(a) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{x}{2}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{2}{x}$

(c) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{2}{x}$

(d) $\frac{x}{2}\div\frac{1}{2}$

(e) $\frac{2}{x}\div 2$

(f) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{x}{2}=\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{2}{x}=\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{1}{x}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}\div\frac{2}{x}=\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{x}{2}=\frac{x}{4}$

(c) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{2}{x}=\frac{2}{x}\times\frac{x}{2}=\frac{2x}{2x}=1$

(d) $\frac{x}{2}\div\frac{1}{2}=\frac{x}{2}\times\frac{2}{1}=\frac{2x}{2}=x$

(e) $\frac{2}{x}\div 2=\frac{2}{x}\times\frac{1}{2}=\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{1}{x}$

(f) $\frac{2}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}=\frac{2}{x}\times\frac{2}{1}=\frac{4}{x}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x}\div\frac{1}{2}=\frac{3}{x}\times\frac{2}{1}=\frac{6}{x}$

##### 3. Simplify

(a) $\frac{5}{4}\times\frac{a}{25}$

(b) $\frac{5}{4}\times\frac{a}{b}$

(c) $\frac{8a}{h^2}\times\frac{b}{16a^2}$

(d) $\frac{9x}{3y}\times\frac{2x}{y^2}$

(e) $\frac{3}{5a}\times\frac{b}{a}$

(f) $\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{x}{y}$

(g) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{x}{x+y}$

(h) $\frac{x-3}{x+4}\times\frac{1}{3x-9}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{5}{4}\times\frac{a}{25}=\frac{5a}{100}=\frac{a}{20}$

(b) $\frac{5}{4}\times\frac{a}{b}=\frac{5a}{4b}$

(c) $\frac{8a}{h^2}\times\frac{b}{16a^2}=\frac{8ab}{h^216a^2}=\frac{b}{2ah^2}$

(d) $\frac{9x}{3y}\times\frac{2x}{y^2}=\frac{18x^2}{3y^3}=\frac{6x^2}{y^3}$

(e) $\frac{3}{5a}\times\frac{b}{a}=\frac{3b}{5a^2}$

(f) $\frac{1}{4}\times\frac{x}{y}=\frac{x}{4y}$

(g) $\frac{1}{3}\times\frac{x}{x+y}=\frac{x}{3(x+y)}=\frac{x}{3x+3y}=\frac{x}{3(x+y)}$

(h) $\frac{x-3}{x+4}\times\frac{1}{3x-9}=\frac{(x-3)\times1}{(x+4)(3x-9)}$
$\frac{x-3}{3x^2-9x+12x-36}=\frac{x-3}{3x^2+3-36}$
$\frac{x-3}{(x-3)(3x+12)}=\frac{1}{3x+12}=\frac{x-3}{3(x+4)}$

$3x^2+3-36$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers

$3 \times -36 = -108$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply yo give -108: $9\times-12=-108$
2. Add to give 3: $-9+12=3$

The numbers are -9 and 12

Step 3: Split the middle term
$3x^2-9x+12x-36$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $3x^2-9x=3x(x-3)$
2. Right side: $12x-36=12(x-3)$

Step 5: Combine into the final answer
$(x-3)(3x+12)=3x^2+12x-9x-36=3x^2+3x-36$

##### 4. Simplify

(a) $\frac{3}{x}\times\frac{xy}{z^3}$

(b) $\frac{(3x+x)}{x}\div\frac{y}{x}$

(c) $\frac{4}{3}\div\frac{16}{x}$

(d) $\frac{a}{hc^2}\times \frac{b^2c}{a}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{3}{x}\times\frac{xy}{z^3}=\frac{3xy}{xz^3}=\frac{3y}{z^3}$

(b) $\frac{(3x+x)}{x}\div\frac{y}{x}=\frac{4x}{x}\times\frac{x}{y}=4\times\frac{x}{y}=\frac{4x}{y}$

(c) $\frac{4}{3}\div\frac{16}{x}=\frac{4}{3}\times\frac{x}{16}=\frac{x}{12}$

(d) $\frac{a}{hc^2}\times \frac{b^2c}{a}=\frac{b^2}{hc}$

##### 5. Simplify

(a) $\frac{x+2}{(x+5)(x+4)}\times\frac{x+5}{x+2}$

(b) $\frac{x-2}{4}\div\frac{x}{16}$

(c) $\frac{12ab}{5ef}\div\frac{4ab^2}{f}$

(d) $\frac{x+3y}{2x}\div\frac{y}{4x^2}$

(e) $\frac{3}{x}\times\frac{3}{y}\times\frac{1}{z}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{x+2}{(x+5)(x+4)}\times\frac{x+5}{x+2}=\frac{1}{x+4}$

(b) $\frac{x-2}{4}\div\frac{x}{16}=\frac{x-2}{4}\times\frac{16}{x-2}=4$

(c) $\frac{12ab}{5ef}\div\frac{4ab^2}{f}=\frac{12ab}{5ef}\times\frac{f}{4ab^2}=\frac{3}{5eb}$

(d) $\frac{x+3y}{2x}\div\frac{y}{4x^2}=\frac{(x+3y)}{2x}\times\frac{4x^2}{y}=\frac{(x+3y)}{1}\times\frac{2x}{y}=\frac{2x^2+6xy}{y}$

(e) $\frac{3}{x}\times\frac{3}{y}\times\frac{1}{z}=\frac{9}{xyz}$

##### 6. Simplify

$\frac{1}{x+1}\times\frac{2x+2}{x+3}$

Solution

$\frac{1}{x+1}\times\frac{2x+2}{x+3}=\frac{2x+2}{(x+1)(x+3)}=\frac{2(x+1)}{(x+1)(x+3)}=\frac{2}{x+3}$

##### 7. Simplify

$\frac{x+1}{x+2}\times\frac{x^2+6x+8}{x^2+4x+3}=\frac{x+1}{x+2}\times\frac{(x+2)(x+4)}{(x+1)(x+3)}=\frac{x+4}{x+3}$

Solution

$\frac{x+1}{x+2}\times\frac{x^2+6x+8}{x^2+4x+3}=$

$x^2+6x+8$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers

$1 \times 8 = 8$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply to give 8
2. Add to give 6:

The numbers are 2 and 4

Step 3: Split the middle term
$x^2+2x+4x+8$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $x^2+2x=x(x+2)$
2. Right side: $4x+8=4(x+2)$

Step 5: Combine into the final answer
$(x+2)(x+4)=x^2+4x+2x+8=x^2+6x+8$

$x^2+4x+3$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers

$1 \times 3 = 3$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

1. Multiply to give 3
2. Add to give 4:

The numbers are 1 and 3

Step 3: Split the middle term
$x^2+1x+3x+3$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $x^2+1x=x(x+1)$
2. Right side: $3x+3=3(x+1)$

Step 5: Combine into the final answer
$(x+1)(x+3)=x^2+3x+1x+3=x^2+4x+3$
