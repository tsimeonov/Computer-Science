##### 1. Simplify

(a) $\frac{9x}{3y}$
(b) $\frac{9x}{x^2}$
(c) $\frac{9xy}{3x}$
(d) $\frac{9xy}{3y}$
(e) $\frac{9xy}{xy}$
(f) $\frac{9xy}{3xy}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{9x}{3y}=\frac{3x}{y}$

(b) $\frac{9x}{x^2}=\frac{9}{x}$

(c) $\frac{9xy}{3x}=3y$

(d) $\frac{9xy}{3y}=3x$

(e) $\frac{9xy}{xy}=9$

(f) $\frac{9xy}{3xy}=3$

##### 2. Simplify

(a) $\frac{15x}{3y}$
(b) $\frac{15x}{5y}$
(c) $\frac{15xy}{x}$
(d) $\frac{15xy}{xy}$
(e) $\frac{x^5}{-x^3}$
(f) $\frac{-y^3}{y^7}$
(g) $\frac{-y}{-y^2}$
(h) $\frac{-y^{-3}}{-y^4}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{15x}{3y}=\frac{5x}{y}$

(b) $\frac{15x}{5y}=\frac{3x}{y}$

(c) $\frac{15xy}{x}=15y$

(d) $\frac{15xy}{xy}=15$

(e) $\frac{x^5}{-x^3}=-\frac{x^5}{x^3}=-x^2$

(f) $\frac{-y^3}{y^7}=-\frac{1}{y^4}$

(g) $\frac{-y}{-y^2}=\frac{1}{y}$

(h) $\frac{-y^{-3}}{-y^4}$
A negative divided by a negative is positive (- / - = +)
$\frac{y^{-3}}{y^4}=\frac{1}{y^3\times y^4}= \frac{1}{y^7}$

##### 3. Simplify the following algebraic fractions

(a) $\frac{4}{12+8x}$
(b) $\frac{5+10x}{5}$
(c) $\frac{2}{4+14x}$
(d) $\frac{2x}{4+14x}$
(e) $\frac{2x}{2+14x}$
(f) $\frac{7}{49x+7y}$
(g) $\frac{7y}{49x+7y}$
(h) $\frac{7x}{49x+7y}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{4}{12+8x}=\frac{1}{3+2x}$

(b) $\frac{5+10x}{5}=\frac{5(1+5x)}{5}=1+5x$

(c) $\frac{2}{4+14x}=\frac{1}{2+7x}$

(d) $\frac{2x}{4+14x}=\frac{2x}{2(2+7x)}=\frac{x}{2+7x}$

(e) $\frac{2x}{2+14x}=\frac{2x}{2(1+7x)}=\frac{x}{1+7x}$

(f) $\frac{7}{49x+7y}=\frac{7}{7(7x+y)}=\frac{1}{7x+y}$

(g) $\frac{7y}{49x+7y}=\frac{7y}{7(7x+y)}=\frac{y}{7x+y}$

(h) $\frac{7x}{49x+7y}=\frac{7x}{7(7x+y)}=\frac{x}{7x+y}$

##### 4. Simplify

(a) $\frac{15x+3}{3}$
(b) $\frac{15x+3}{3x+6y}$
(c) $\frac{12}{4x+8}$
(d) $\frac{12x}{4xy+8x}$
(e) $\frac{13x}{x^2+5x}$
(f) $\frac{17y}{9y^2+4y}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{15x+3}{3}=5x+1$

(b) $\frac{15x+3}{3x+6y}=\frac{3(5x+1)}{3(x+2y)}=\frac{5x+1}{x+2y}$

(c) $\frac{12}{4x+8}=\frac{3}{x+2}$

(d) $\frac{12x}{4xy+8x}=\frac{12x}{4x(y+2)}=\frac{3x}{y+2}$

(e) $\frac{13x}{x^2+5x}=\frac{13x}{x(x+5)}=\frac{13}{x+5}$

(f) $\frac{17y}{9y^2+4y}=\frac{17y}{y(9y+4)}=\frac{17}{9y+4}$

##### 5. Simplify

(c) $\frac{2x+8}{x^2+2x-8}=\frac{2(x+4)}{(x-2)(x+4)}=\frac{2}{x-2}$

Step 1: Multiply the first and last numbers
$1 \times (-8)=-8$

Step 2: Find the magic numbers

2. Add to give 2:

The numbers are 4 and -2

Step 3: Split the middle term
$x^2-2x+4x-8$

Step 4: Factor the grouping

1. Left side: $x^2-2x=x(x-2)$
2. Right side: $4x-8=4(x-2)$

Step 5: Combine into the final answer

$(x-2)(x+4)=x^2+4x-2x-8=x^2+2x-8$
