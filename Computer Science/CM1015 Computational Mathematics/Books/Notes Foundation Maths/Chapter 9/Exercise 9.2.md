#### 9.2 Exercise

##### 1. Simplify

(a) $\frac{9x}{3y}$
(b) $\frac{9x}{x^2}$
(c) $\frac{9xy}{3x}$
(d) $\frac{9xy}{3y}$
(e) $\frac{9xy}{xy}$
(f) $\frac{9xy}{3xy}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{9x}{3y}=\frac{3x}{y}$

(b) $\frac{9x}{x^2}=\frac{9}{x}$

(c) $\frac{9xy}{3x}=3y$

(d) $\frac{9xy}{3y}=3x$

(e) $\frac{9xy}{xy}=9$

(f) $\frac{9xy}{3xy}=3$

##### 2. Simplify

(a) $\frac{15x}{3y}$
(b) $\frac{15x}{5y}$
(c) $\frac{15xy}{x}$
(d) $\frac{15xy}{xy}$
(e) $\frac{x^5}{-x^3}$
(f) $\frac{-y^3}{y^7}$
(g) $\frac{-y}{-y^2}$
(h) $\frac{-y^{-3}}{-y^4}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{15x}{3y}=\frac{5x}{y}$

(b) $\frac{15x}{5y}=\frac{3x}{y}$

(c) $\frac{15xy}{x}=15y$

(d) $\frac{15xy}{xy}=15$

(e) $\frac{x^5}{-x^3}=-\frac{x^5}{x^3}=-x^2$

(f) $\frac{-y^3}{y^7}=-\frac{1}{y^4}$

(g) $\frac{-y}{-y^2}=\frac{1}{y}$

(h) $\frac{-y^{-3}}{-y^4}$
A negative divided by a negative is positive (- / - = +)
$\frac{y^{-3}}{y^4}=\frac{1}{y^3\times y^4}= \frac{1}{y^7}$
