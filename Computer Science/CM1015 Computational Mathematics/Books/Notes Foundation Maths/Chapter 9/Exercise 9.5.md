##### 1. Find the partial fractions of the following:

(a) $\frac{7x+18}{(x+2)(x+3)}$

(b) $\frac{2x-7}{x^2+5x+4}$

(c) $\frac{-9}{2x^2+15x+18}$

(d) $\frac{5x-11}{x^2-5x+4}$

(e) $\frac{3x+11}{2x^2+3x-2}$

Solution:

(a) $\frac{7x+18}{(x+2)(x+3)}$

Step 1: Set up the partial fraction form

$\frac{7x+18}{(x+2)(x+3)}=\frac{A}{x+2}+\frac{B}{x+3}$

Step 2: Clear the denominator

$7x+18=A(x+3)+B(x+2)$

Step 3: Solve for constants A and B

To find A, let x = -2:

$7(-2)+18=A(-2+3)+B(-2+2)=-14+18=A(1)+0=4$

To find B, let x = -3:

$7(-3)+18=A(-3+3)+B(-3+2)=-21+18=0+B(-1)=-3=-B=> B=3$

FInal answer: $\frac{4}{x+2}+\frac{3}{x+3}$

(b) $\frac{2x-7}{x^2+5x+4}$

2. Right side: $4x+14= 4(x+1)$

Step 5: Combine into the final answer
$(x+1)(x+4)=x^2+4x+1x+4=x^2+4x+4$

$\frac{2x-7}{(x+1)(x+4)}$

$\frac{2x-7}{(x+1)(x+4)}=\frac{A}{x+1}+\frac{B}{x+4}$

To find B, let x = -4
$2(-4)-7=A(-4+4)+B(-4+1)=-8-7=B(-3)=5$

Final answer: $\frac{-3}{x+1}+\frac{5}{x+4}$

##### 2. Find the partial fractions of the following:

$\frac{x+21}{2(2x+3)(3x-2)}$

Step 1: Set up the partial fraction form

$\frac{x+21}{(2x+3)(3x-2)}=\frac{A}{2x+3}+\frac{B}{3x-2}$

Step 2: Clear the denominator

$x+21=A(3x-2)+B(2x+3)$

To find A, let x = $-\frac{3}{2}$
$(-\frac{3}{2})+21=A(3(-\frac{3}{2})-2)+B(2x+3)$
$(-\frac{3}{2})+21=A(3(-\frac{3}{2})-2)+B(0)$
$(-\frac{3}{2}+\frac{42}{2})=A(-\frac{9}{2}-2)$
$\frac{39}{2}=A(-\frac{9}{2}-\frac{4}{2})$
$\frac{39}{2}=A(-\frac{13}{2})$
$39=-13A$
$A=\frac{39}{-13}=-3$

To find B, let $\frac{2}{3}$
$\frac{2}{3}+21=A(3(\frac{2}{3})-2)+B(2(\frac{2}{3})+3)$
$\frac{2}{3}+\frac{63}{3}=A(0)+B(\frac{4}{3}+3)$
$\frac{65}{3}=B(\frac{13}{3})$
$65=13B$
$B=\frac{65}{13}=5$

Final answer
$\frac{x+21}{2(2x+3)(x-2)}=\frac{-3}{2(2x+3)}+\frac{5}{2(3x-2)}$

##### 3. Find the partial fractions of the following:

(a) $\frac{x-35}{x^2-25}$

(c) $\frac{5x+4}{-x^2-x+2}$

(d) $\frac{12x-5}{9x^2-6x+1}$

Solution

<b> (a) $\frac{x-35}{x^2-25}=\frac{x-35}{(x-5)(x+5)}$</b>

Step 1: Set up the partial fraction from

$\frac{x-35}{(x-5)(x+5)}=\frac{A}{x-5}+\frac{B}{x+5}$

Step 2: Clear the denominator

$x-35=A(x+5)+B(x-5)$

To find A, let x = 5

$5-35=A(5-5)+B(5+5)$
$-30=A(0)+B(10)$
$B=\frac{-30}{10}=-3$

To find B, let x = -5

$-5-35=A(-5-5)+B(-5+5)$
$-40=A(-10)+B(0)$
$A=\frac{-40}{-10}=4$

Final answer

$\frac{x-35}{x^2-25}=\frac{-3}{x-5}+\frac{4}{x+5}$

$\frac{x-4}{(x-3)(x-3)}$
