#### 9.5 Exercise

##### 1. Find the partial fractions of the following:

(a) $\frac{7x+18}{(x+2)(x+3)}$

(b) $\frac{2x-7}{x^2+5x+4}$

(c) $\frac{-9}{2x^2+15x+18}$

(d) $\frac{5x-11}{x^2-5x+4}$

(e) $\frac{3x+11}{2x^2+3x-2}$

Solution:

(a) $\frac{7x+18}{(x+2)(x+3)}$

Step 1: Set up the partial fraction form

$\frac{7x+18}{(x+2)(x+3)}=\frac{A}{x+2}+\frac{B}{x+3}$

Step 2: Clear the denominator

$7x+18=A(x+3)+B(x+2)$

Step 3: Solve for constants A and B

To find A, let x = -2:

$7(-2)+18=A(-2+3)+B(-2+2)=-14+18=A(1)+0=4$

To find B, let x = -3:

$7(-3)+18=A(-3+3)+B(-3+2)=-21+18=0+B(-1)=-3=-B=> B=3$

FInal answer: $\frac{4}{x+2}+\frac{3}{x+3}$

Step 5: Combine into the final answer

$\frac{2x-7}{(x+1)(x+4)}$

Step 1: Set up the partial fraction form

$\frac{2x-7}{(x+1)(x+4)}=\frac{A}{x+1}+\frac{B}{x+4}$

Step 2: Clear the denominator

$2x-7=A(x+4)+B(x+1)$

Step 3: Solve for constants A and B

To find A, let x = -1
$2(-1)-7=A(-1+4)+B(-1+1)=-2-7=A(3)=-9=-3$

To find B, let x = -4
$2(-4)-7=A(-4+4)+B(-4+1)=-8-7=B(-3)=5$

Final answer: $\frac{-3}{x+1}+\frac{5}{x+4}$
