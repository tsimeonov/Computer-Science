#### 9.4 Exercise

##### 1. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{z}{2}+\frac{z}{3}$

(b) $\frac{x}{3}+\frac{x}{4}$

(c) $\frac{y}{5}+\frac{y}{25}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{z}{2}+\frac{z}{3}=\frac{3z}{6}+\frac{2z}{6}=\frac{5z}{6}$

(b) $\frac{x}{3}+\frac{x}{4}=\frac{4x}{12}+\frac{3x}{12}=\frac{7x}{12}$

(c) $\frac{y}{5}+\frac{y}{25}=\frac{5y}{25}+\frac{y}{25}=\frac{6y}{25}$

##### 2. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{x}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}+x$

(c) $\frac{1}{3}+y$

(d) $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{y}$

(e) $8+\frac{1}{y}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{x}=\frac{x}{2x}+\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{x+2}{2x}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}+x=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2x}{2}=\frac{1+2x}{2}$

(c) $\frac{1}{3}+y=\frac{1}{3}+\frac{3y}{3}=\frac{1+3y}{3}$

(d) $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{y}{3y}+\frac{3}{3y}=\frac{y+3}{3y}$

(e) $8+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{8y}{y}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{8y+1}{y}$
