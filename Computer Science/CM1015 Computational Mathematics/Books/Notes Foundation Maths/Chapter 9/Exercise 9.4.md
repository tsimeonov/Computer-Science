#### 9.4 Exercise

##### 1. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{z}{2}+\frac{z}{3}$

(b) $\frac{x}{3}+\frac{x}{4}$

(c) $\frac{y}{5}+\frac{y}{25}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{z}{2}+\frac{z}{3}=\frac{3z}{6}+\frac{2z}{6}=\frac{5z}{6}$

(b) $\frac{x}{3}+\frac{x}{4}=\frac{4x}{12}+\frac{3x}{12}=\frac{7x}{12}$

(c) $\frac{y}{5}+\frac{y}{25}=\frac{5y}{25}+\frac{y}{25}=\frac{6y}{25}$

##### 2. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{x}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}+x$

(c) $\frac{1}{3}+y$

(d) $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{y}$

(e) $8+\frac{1}{y}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{x}=\frac{x}{2x}+\frac{2}{2x}=\frac{x+2}{2x}$

(b) $\frac{1}{2}+x=\frac{1}{2}+\frac{2x}{2}=\frac{1+2x}{2}$

(c) $\frac{1}{3}+y=\frac{1}{3}+\frac{3y}{3}=\frac{1+3y}{3}$

(d) $\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{y}{3y}+\frac{3}{3y}=\frac{y+3}{3y}$

(e) $8+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{8y}{y}+\frac{1}{y}=\frac{8y+1}{y}$

---

##### 3. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{5}{x}-\frac{1}{2}$

(b) $\frac{5}{x}+2$

(c) $\frac{3}{x}-\frac{1}{3}$

(d) $\frac{x}{3}-\frac{1}{2}$

(e) $\frac{3}{x}+\frac{1}{3}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{5}{x}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{5\times(2)}{2x}-\frac{1\times(x)}{2x}=\frac{10-x}{2x}$

(b) $\frac{5}{x}+2=\frac{5}{x}+\frac{2\times(x)}{x}=\frac{5+2x}{x}$

(c) $\frac{3}{x}-\frac{1}{3}=\frac{3\times(3)}{3x}-\frac{1\times(x)}{3x}=\frac{9-x}{3x}$

(d) $\frac{x}{3}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{x\times(2)}{6}-\frac{1\times(3)}{6}=\frac{2x-3}{6}$

(e) $\frac{3}{x}+\frac{1}{3}=\frac{3\times(3)}{3x}+\frac{1\times(x)}{3x}=\frac{9+x}{3x}$

##### 4. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{3}{x}+\frac{4}{y}$

(b) $\frac{3}{x^2}+\frac{4y}{x}$

(c) $\frac{4ab}{x}+\frac{3ab}{2y}$

(d) $\frac{4xy}{a}+\frac{3xy}{2b}$

(e) $\frac{3}{x}-\frac{6}{2x}$

(f) $\frac{3x}{2y}-\frac{7y}{4x}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x+y}-\frac{2}{y}$

(h) $\frac{1}{a+b}-\frac{1}{a-b}$

(i) $2x+\frac{1}{2x}$

(j) $2x-\frac{1}{2x}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{3}{x}+\frac{4}{y}=\frac{3y}{xy}+\frac{4x}{xy}=\frac{3y+4x}{xy}$

(b) $\frac{3}{x^2}+\frac{4y}{x}=\frac{3}{x^2}+\frac{4xy}{x^2}=\frac{3+4xy}{x^2}$

(c) $\frac{4ab}{x}+\frac{3ab}{2y}=\frac{8aby}{2xy}+\frac{3abx}{2xy}=\frac{8aby+3abx}{2xy}$

(d) $\frac{4xy}{a}+\frac{3xy}{2b}=\frac{8xyb}{2ab}+\frac{3xya}{2ab}=\frac{8xyb+3xya}{2ab}$

(e) $\frac{3}{x}-\frac{6}{2x}=\frac{6}{2x}-\frac{6}{2x}=0$

(f) $\frac{3x}{2y}-\frac{7y}{4x}=\frac{6x^2}{4xy}-\frac{7y^2}{4xy}=\frac{6x^2-7y^2}{4xy}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x+y}-\frac{2}{y}=\frac{3y}{y(x+y)}-\frac{2(xy)}{y(x+y)}=\frac{3y-2(xy)}{y(x+y)}=\frac{3y-2x-2y}{y(x+y)}=\frac{y-2x}{y(x+y)}$

(h) $\frac{1}{a+b}-\frac{1}{a-b}=\frac{a-b}{(a+b)(a-b)}-\frac{a+b}{(a+b)(a-b)}=$

$\frac{(a-b)-(a+b)}{(a-b)(a+b)}=\frac{a-b-a-b}{a^2-b^2}=\frac{-2b}{a^2-b^2}$

(i) $2x+\frac{1}{2x}=\frac{2x\times(2x)}{2x}+\frac{1}{2x}=\frac{4x^2+1}{2x}$

(j) $2x-\frac{1}{2x}=\frac{2x\times(2x)}{2x}-\frac{1}{2x}=\frac{4x^2-1}{2x}$

##### 5. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

##### 6. Express each of the following as a single fraction:

(a) $\frac{1}{x+1}+\frac{1}{x+2}$

(b) $\frac{1}{x-1}+\frac{2}{x+3}$

(c) $\frac{3}{x+5}+\frac{1}{x+4}$

(d) $\frac{1}{x-2}+\frac{3}{x-4}$

(e) $\frac{3}{2x+1}+\frac{1}{x+1}$

(f) $\frac{3}{1-2x}+\frac{1}{x}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x+1}+\frac{4}{(x+1)^2}$

(h) $\frac{1}{x-1}+\frac{1}{(x-1)^2}$

Solution

(a) $\frac{1}{x+1}+\frac{1}{x+2}=\frac{(x+2)}{(x+1)(x+2)}+\frac{(x+1)}{(x+1)(x+2)}=$

$\frac{(x+2)+(x+1)}{(x+1)(x+2)}=\frac{2x+3}{(x+1)(x+2)}$

(b) $\frac{1}{x-1}+\frac{2}{x+3}=\frac{(x+3)}{(x-1)(x+3)}+\frac{2\times(x-1)}{(x-1)(x+3)}=\frac{x+3+2x-2}{(x-1)(x+3)}=\frac{3x+1}{(x-1)(x+3)}$

(c) $\frac{3}{x+5}+\frac{1}{x+4}=\frac{3\times(x+4)}{(x+5)(x+4)}+\frac{x+5}{(x+5)(x+4)}=\frac{3x+12+x+5}{(x+5)(x+4)}=\frac{4x+17}{(x+5)(x+4)}$

(d) $\frac{1}{x-2}+\frac{3}{x-4}=\frac{x-4}{(x-2)(x-4)}+\frac{3\times(x-2)}{(x-2)(x-4)}=$

$\frac{x-4+3x-6}{(x-2)(x-4)}=\frac{4x-10}{(x-2)(x-4)}=\frac{2(2x-5)}{(x-2)(x-4)}$

(e) $\frac{3}{2x+1}+\frac{1}{x+1}=\frac{3\times(x+1)}{(2x+1)(x+1)}+\frac{2x+1}{(2x+1)(x+1)}$

$\frac{3x+3}{(2x+1)(x+1)}+\frac{2x+1}{(2x+1)(x+1)}=\frac{5x+4}{(2x+1)(x+1)}$

(f) $\frac{3}{1-2x}+\frac{1}{x}=\frac{3x}{(1-2x)x}+\frac{1-2x}{(1-2x)x}=\frac{3x+1-2x}{(1-2x)x}=$

$\frac{x+1}{(1-2x)x}$

(g) $\frac{3}{x+1}+\frac{4}{(x+1)^2}=\frac{3\times(x+1)}{(x+1)^2}+\frac{4}{(x+1)^2}=$

$\frac{3x+3}{(x+1)^2}+\frac{4}{(x+1)^2}=\frac{3x+7}{(x+1)^2}$

(h) $\frac{1}{x-1}+\frac{1}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{x-1}{(x-1)^2}+\frac{1}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{x}{(x-1)^2}$
