### 5.2 Power of Indices

#### 5.11 Evaluate

a) $(1 + 2^3)^2$

b) $\frac{2^3-1}{2^2+3}$

---

Solution

a) $(1 + 2^3)^2$

- $1 + 2^3 = 1 + 8 = 9$
- $(1 + 2^3)^2 = 9^2 = 81$

b) $\frac{2^3-1}{2^2+3}$

- $\frac{2^3-1}{2^2+3}$
- $2^3-1 = 8^2 - 1 = 7$
- $2^2+3 = 2^2 + 3 = 7$
- $\frac{7}{7} = 1$

#### 5.12 Simplify

$x^2y^2 + 3(xy)^2$

---

Solution

- The bracketed expression is simplified first

$(xy)^2 = (xy)(xy) = x^2y^2$

- So we have

$x^2y^2 + 3(xy)^2 = x^2y^2 + 3x^2y^2$

- $x^2y^2 + 3(xy)^2 = 4x^2y^2$
