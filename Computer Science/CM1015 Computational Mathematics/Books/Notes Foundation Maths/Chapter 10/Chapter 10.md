##### 1. Make x the subject of the following formulae

(a) $y=13x-18$

(b) $y=\frac{13}{x+18}$

(c) $y=\frac{13}{x}+18$

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) $y=13x-18$</h6>

$y+18=13x-18+18$

$y+18=13x$

$\frac{y+18}{13}=\frac{13x}{13}$

$x=\frac{y+18}{13}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $y=\frac{13}{x+18}$</h6>

$y(x+18)=\frac{13}{x+18}\times x+18$

$y(x+18)=13$

$yx+18y=13$

$yx+18y-18y=13-18y$

$yx=13-18y$

$\frac{yx}{y}=\frac{13-18y}{y}$

$x=\frac{13-18y}{y}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $y=\frac{13}{x}+18$</h6>

$y-18=\frac{13}{x}+18-18$

$y-18=\frac{13}{x}$

$x(y-18)=\frac{13}{x}\times x$

$xy-18x=13$

$x(y-18)=13$

$\frac{x(y-18)}{y-18}=\frac{13}{y-18}$

$x=\frac{13}{y-18}$

##### 2. Transpose the following formulae

(a) $y=\frac{7+x}{14}$ for x

(b) $E=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ for v

(c) $8x-13y=12$ for y

(d) $y=\frac{x^2}{2g}$ for x

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) $y=\frac{7+x}{14}$ for x</h6>

$y=\frac{7+x}{14}$

$14y=\frac{7+x}{14}\times 14$

$14y=7+x$

$14y-7=7+x-7$

$14y-7=x$

$x=14y-7$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $E=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$ for v</h6>

$E=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$

$2E=\frac{1}{2}mv^2\times 2$

$2E=mv^2$

$\frac{2E}{m}=\frac{mv^2}{m}$

$\frac{2E}{m}=v^2$

$v=\sqrt{\frac{2E}{m}}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $8x-13y=12$ for y</h6>

$8x-13y=12$

$8x-12=13y$

$\frac{8x-12}{13}=\frac{13y}{13}$

$y=\frac{8x-12}{13}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) $y=\frac{x^2}{2g}$ for x</h6>

$y=\frac{x^2}{2g}$

$2gy=\frac{x^2}{2g}\times 2g$

$x^2=2gy$

$x=\sqrt{2gy}$

---

##### 3. Make x the subject of the folrmula $v=k/\sqrt{x}$

$v=\frac{k}{\sqrt{x}}$

$v\times\sqrt{x}=\frac{k}{\sqrt{x}}\times \sqrt{x}$

$v\times\sqrt{x}=k$

$\frac{v\times\sqrt{x}}{v}=\frac{k}{v}$

$\sqrt{x}=\frac{k}{v}$

$x=\frac{k^2}{v^2}$

---

##### 4. Transpose the formula $V= πr^2h$ for h

$V= πr^2h$

$\frac{V}{πr^2}= \frac{πr^2h}{πr^2}$

$h=\frac{V}{πr^2}$

---

##### 5. Make r the subject of the formula $y=H+Cr$

$y=H+Cr$

$y-H=H+Cr-H$

$y-H=Cr$

$\frac{y-H}{C}=\frac{Cr}{C}$

$r=\frac{y-H}{C}$

---

##### 6. Transpose the formula $s=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2$ for a

$s=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2$

$s-ut=ut+\frac{1}{2}at^2-ut$

$s-ut=\frac{1}{2}at^2$

$2(s-ut)=\frac{1}{2}at^2\times2$

$2(s-ut)=at^2$

$\frac{2(s-ut)}{t^2}=\frac{at^2}{t^2}$

$a=\frac{2(s-ut)}{t^2}$

---

##### 7. Transpose the formula $v^2=u^2+2as$ for u

$v^2=u^2+2as$

$v^2-2as=u^2+2as-2as$

$u^2=v^2-2as$

$u=\sqrt{v^2-2as}$

---

##### 8. Transose the formula $k=pv^3$ for v

$k=pv^3$

$\frac{k}{p}=\frac{pv^3}{p}$

$v^3=\frac{k}{p}$

$v=\sqrt[3]{\frac{k}{p}}$
