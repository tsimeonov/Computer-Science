##### Example 1

Transpose this to make r the subject

$C=2πr$

<details>
  <summary>Solution</summary>
  
$C=2πr$

$\frac{C}{2π}=\frac{2πr}{2π}$

$\frac{C}{2π}=r$

$r=\frac{C}{2π}$

</details>

##### Example 2

Transpose the formula $y=3(x+7)$ for x

<details>
  <summary>Solution</summary>

$y=3(x+7)$

$\frac{y}{3}=\frac{3(x+7)}{3}$

$\frac{y}{3}=x+7$

Subtract 7 from both sides

$\frac{y}{3}-7=x+7-7$

$\frac{y}{3}-7=x$

$x= \frac{y}{3}-7$

</details>
