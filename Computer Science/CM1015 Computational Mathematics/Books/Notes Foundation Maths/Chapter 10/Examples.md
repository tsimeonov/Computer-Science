##### Example 1

Transpose this to make r the subject

$C=2πr$

<details>
  <summary>Solution</summary>
  
$C=2πr$

$\frac{C}{2π}=\frac{2πr}{2π}$

$\frac{C}{2π}=r$

$r=\frac{C}{2π}$
