#### 10.1 Exercise

##### 1. Transpose each of the following formulae to make x the subject:

(a) $y=3x=$

(b) $y=\frac{1}{x}$

(c) $y=7x-5$

(d) $y=\frac{1}{2}x-7$

(e) $y=\frac{1}{2x}$

(f) $y=\frac{1}{2x+1}$

(g) $y=\frac{1}{2x}+1$

(h) $y=18x-21$

(i) $y=19-8x$

Solution

(a) $y=3x=$
$\frac{y}{3}=\frac{3x}{3}$ Divide both sides by 3
$x=\frac{y}{3}$

(b) $y=\frac{1}{x}$

Step 1: Multiply both sides by x to clear it from the denominator
$y\times x=\frac{1}{x}\times x$
$xy=1$

Step 2: Divide both sudes by y to isolate x
$\frac{xy}{y}=\frac{1}{y}$

(c) $y=7x-5$

(d) $y=\frac{1}{2}x-7$

(e) $y=\frac{1}{2x}$

(f) $y=\frac{1}{2x+1}$

(g) $y=\frac{1}{2x}+1$

(h) $y=18x-21$

(i) $y=19-8x$
