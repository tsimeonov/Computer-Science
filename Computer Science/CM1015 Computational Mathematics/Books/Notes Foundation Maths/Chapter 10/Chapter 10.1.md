#### 10.1 Exercise

##### 1. Transpose each of the following formulae to make x the subject:

(a) $y=3x=$

(b) $y=\frac{1}{x}$

(c) $y=7x-5$

(d) $y=\frac{1}{2}x-7$

(e) $y=\frac{1}{2x}$

(f) $y=\frac{1}{2x+1}$

(g) $y=\frac{1}{2x}+1$

(h) $y=18x-21$

(i) $y=19-8x$

Solution

(a) $y=3x$

<h6>Divide both sides by 3</h6>
$\frac{y}{3}=\frac{3x}{3}$
$x=\frac{y}{3}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $y=\frac{1}{x}$</h6>

Step 1: Multiply both sides by x to clear it from the denominator

$y\times x=\frac{1}{x}\times x$
$xy=1$

Step 2: Divide both sudes by y to isolate x

$\frac{xy}{y}=\frac{1}{y}$
$x=\frac{1}{y}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $y=7x-5$</h6>

$y+5=7x-5+5$

$y+5=7x$

$\frac{y+5}{7}=\frac{7x}{7}$

$x=\frac{y+5}{7}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) $y=\frac{1}{2}x-7$</h6>

$y+7=\frac{1}{2}x-7+7$

$y+7=\frac{1}{2}x$

Multiply by 2 both sides

$2(y+7)=\frac{1}2{x}\times 2$
$x = 2(y+7)$
$x = 2y+14$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) $y=\frac{1}{2x}$</h6>

$y\times (2x)=\frac{1}{2x} \times (2x)$

$2xy=1$

$\frac{2xy}{2y}=\frac{1}{2y}$

$x=\frac{1}{2y}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(f) $y=\frac{1}{2x+1}$</h6>

$y \times (2x+1)=\frac{1}{2x+1}\times (2x+1)$

$2xy+y=1$

$2xy+y-y=1-y$

$2xy=1-y$

$\frac{2xy}{2y}=\frac{1-y}{2y}$

$x=\frac{1-y}{2y}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(g) $y=\frac{1}{2x}+1$</h6>

$y-1=\frac{1}{2x}+1-1$

$y-1=\frac{1}{2x}$

$2x\times(y-1)=\frac{1}{2x}\times(2x)$

$2x\times(y-1)=1$

$\frac{2x\times(y-1)}{2(y-1)}=\frac{1}{2(y-1)}$

$x=\frac{1}{2(y-1)}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(h) $y=18x-21$</h6>

$y+21=18x-21+21$

$y+21=18x$

$\frac{y+21}{18}=\frac{18x}{18}$

$x= \frac{y+21}{18}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(i) $y=19-8x$</h6>

$y+8x=19-8x+8x$

$y+8x=19$

$y+8x=19$

$y+8x-y=19-y$

$8x=19-y$

$\frac{8x}{8}=\frac{19-y}{8}$

$x=\frac{19-y}{8}$

##### 2. Transpose y=mx+c

(a) for m
(b) for x
(c) for c

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) y=mx+c, for m</h6>

$y-c=mx+c-c$

$y-c=mx$

$\frac{y-c}{x}=\frac{mx}{x}$

$m=\frac{y-c}{x}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) y=mx+c, for x</h6>

$y-c=mx+c-c$

$y-c=mx$

$\frac{y-c}{m}=\frac{mx}{m}$

$x=\frac{y-c}{m}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) y=mx+c, for c</h6>

$y-mx=mx+c$

$y-mx=c$

$c=y-mx$

##### 3. Transpose the following formulae for x:

(a) $y=13(x-2)$

(b) $y=x(1+\frac{1}{x})$

(c) $y=a+t(x-3)$

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) $y=13(x-2)$</h6>

$y=13x-26$

$y+26=13x-26+26$

$y+26=13x$

$\frac{y+26}{13}=\frac{13x}{13}$

$x= \frac{y+26}{13}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $y=x(1+\frac{1}{x})$</h6>

$y=x\times1+x\times\frac{1}{x}$

Since $x\times\frac{1}{x}=1$

$y=x+1$

$y-1=x+1-1$

$x=y-1$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $y=a+t(x-3)$</h6>

$y=a+tx-3t$

$y+3t=a+tx-3t+3t$

$y+3t=a+tx$

$y+3t-a=a+tx-a$

$y+3t-a=tx$

$\frac{y+3t-a}{t}=\frac{tx}{t}$

$x= \frac{y+3t-a}{t}$

---

##### 4. Transpose each of the following formulae to make the given variable the subject:

(a) $y=7x+11$, for x

(b) $V=IR$, for I

(c) $V=\frac{4}{3}πr^2$, for r

(d) $F=ma$, for m

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) $y=7x+11$, for x</h6>

$y-11=7x+11-11$

$y-11=7x$

$\frac{y-11}{7}=\frac{7x}{7}$

$x=\frac{y-11}{7}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $V=IR$, for I</h6>

$\frac{V}{R}=\frac{IR}{R}$

$I=\frac{V}{R}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $V=\frac{4}{3}πr^2$, for r</h6>

$3V=\frac{4}{3}πr^2\times3$

$3V=4πr^2$

$\frac{3V}{4π}=\frac{4πr^2}{4π}$

$r^2=\frac{3V}{4π}$

$r=\sqrt{\frac{3V}{4π}}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) $F=ma$, for m</h6>

$\frac{F}{a}=\frac{ma}{a}$

$m=\frac{F}{a}$

---

##### 5. Make n the subject of the formula $l=a+(n-1)d$

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">$l=a+(n-1)d$</h6>

$l=a+nd-d$

$l-a+d=a+nd-d$

$l-a+d=nd$

$\frac{l-a+d}{d}=\frac{nd}{d}$
$n=\frac{l-a+d}{d}$

---

##### 6. If $m=n+t\sqrt{x}$, find an expression for x

Solution

$m=n+t\sqrt{x}$

$m-n=n+t\sqrt{x}-n$

$m-n=t\sqrt{x}$

$\frac{m-n}{t}=\frac{t\sqrt{x}}{t}$
$\frac{m-n}{t}=\sqrt{x}$

$x=(\frac{m-n}{t})^2$

##### 7. Make x the subject of the following formulae

(a) $y=1-x^2$

(b) $y=\frac{1}{1-x^2}$

(c) $y=\frac{1-x^2}{2+x^2}$

Solution:

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) $y=1-x^2$</h6>

Eliminate the negative sign by multiplying both sides by -1

$-(y-1)=-(-x^2)$

$y-1=x^2$

$x=\sqrt{y-1}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $y=\frac{1}{1-x^2}$</h6>

$y(1-x^2)=\frac{1}{1-x^2}\times 1-x^2$

$y(1-x^2)=1$

$\frac{y(1-x^2)}{y}=\frac{1}{y}$

$1-x^2=\frac{1}{y}$

$x^2=1-\frac{1}{y}$

$x=\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{y}}$

---

$y(1-x^2)=\frac{1}{1-x^2}\times1-x^2$

$y(1-x^2)=1$

$\frac{y(1-x^2)}{y}=\frac{1}{y}$

$1-x^2=\frac{1}{y}$

$1-\frac{1}{y}=x^2$

$x^2=1-\frac{1}{y}$

$x=\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{y}}$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $y=\frac{1-x^2}{2+x^2}$</h6>
