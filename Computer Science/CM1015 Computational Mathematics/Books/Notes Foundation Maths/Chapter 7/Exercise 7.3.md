##### 2. Remove the brackets from

- $5(x-y)$
- $19(x+3y)$
- $8(a+b)$
- $(5+x)y$
- $12(x+4)$
- $17(x-9)$
- $-(a+2b)$
- $\frac{1}{2}(2x+1)$
- $-3m(-2+4m+3n)$

Solution

- $5(x-y) = 5x-5y$
- $19(x+3y) = 19x+57y$
- $8(a+b)=8a+8b$
- $(5+x)y=5y+xy$
- $12(x+4)=12x+48$
- $17(x-9)=17x-153$
- $-(a+2b)=-a-2b$
- $\frac{1}{2}(2x+1) = x + 0.5$
- $-3m(-2+4m+3n) = 6m+-12m^2-9mn$

##### 3. Remove the brackets and simplify the expressions

- $18-13(x+2)$
- $x(x+y)$

Solution

- $18-13(x+2)= 18-13x-26=-13x-8$
- $x(x+y)=x^2+xy$

##### 4. Remove the brackets and simplify the following expressions

- $(x+1)(x+6)$
- $(x+4)(x+5)$
- $(x-2)(x+3)$
- $(x+6)(x-1)$
- $(x+y)(m+n)$
- $(4+y)(3+x)$
- $(5-x)(5+x)$
- $(17x+2)(3x-5)$

Solution

- $(x+1)(x+6)=x^2+6x+x+6= x^2+7+6$
  <br>
- $(x+4)(x+5)= x^2+5x+4x+20=x^2+9x+20$
  <br>

- $(x-2)(x+3)=x^2+3x-2x+6=x^2+x-6$
  <br>

- $(x+6)(x-1)=x^2-x+6x+6=x^2+5x-6$
  <br>

- $(x+y)(m+n)=xm+xn+ym+yn$
  <br>

- $(4+y)(3+x)=12+4x+3y+xy$
  <br>

- $(5-x)(5+x)=25+5x-5x+x^2=25-x^2$
  <br>

- $(17x+2)(3x-5)=51x^2-85x+6x-10=51x^2-79x-10$

##### 5. Remove the brackets and simplify the following expressions

- $(x+3)(x-7)$
- $(2x-1)(3x+7)$
- $(4x+1)(4x-1)$
- $(x+3)(x-3)$
- $(2-x)(3+2x)$

Solution

- $(x+3)(x-7)=x^2-7x+3x-21=x^2-4x-21$
  <br>

- $(2x-1)(3x+7)=6x^2+11x-7$
  <br>

- $(4x+1)(4x-1)=16x^2-4x+4-1=16x^2-1$
  <br>

- $(x+3)(x-3)=x^2-3x+3x-9=x^2-9$
  <br>

- $(2-x)(3+2x)=6+4x-3x-2x^2=6+x-2x^2$

##### 6. Remove the brackets from

- $\frac{1}{2}(x+2y)+\frac{7}{2}(4x-y)$
- $\frac{3}{4}(x-1)+\frac{1}{4}(2x+8)$

Solution

- $\frac{1}{2}(x+2y)+\frac{7}{2}(4x-y)$
  $\frac{1}{2}(x)=\frac{1}{2}x$
  $\frac{1}{2}(2y)=\frac{1}{2}2y=y$
  $\frac{7}{2}(4x)=\frac{28x}{2}=14x$
  $\frac{7}{2}(-y)=-\frac{7}{2}y$
  $\frac{1}{2}x+y+14x-\frac{7}{2}y$
  Combine the x terms
  $\frac{1}{2}x+14x = 0.5x+14x = 14.5x$
  Combine the y terms
  $1y-\frac{7}{2}y = -\frac{5}{2}y=-2.5y$
  $14.5x-2.5y$

  <br>

- $\frac{3}{4}(x-1)+\frac{1}{4}(2x+8)$
  $\frac{3}{4}x-\frac{1}{4}+2$
