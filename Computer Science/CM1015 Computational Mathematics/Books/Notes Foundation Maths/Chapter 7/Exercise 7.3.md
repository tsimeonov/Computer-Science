##### 2. Remove the brackets from

- $5(x-y)$
- $19(x+3y)$
- $8(a+b)$
- $(5+x)y$
- $12(x+4)$
- $17(x-9)$
- $-(a+2b)$
- $\frac{1}{2}(2x+1)$
- $-3m(-2+4m+3n)$

Solution

- $5(x-y) = 5x-5y$
- $19(x+3y) = 19x+57y$
- $8(a+b)=8a+8b$
- $(5+x)y=5y+xy$
- $12(x+4)=12x+48$
- $17(x-9)=17x-153$
- $-(a+2b)=-a-2b$
- $\frac{1}{2}(2x+1) = x + 0.5$
- $-3m(-2+4m+3n) = 6m+-12m^2-9mn$
