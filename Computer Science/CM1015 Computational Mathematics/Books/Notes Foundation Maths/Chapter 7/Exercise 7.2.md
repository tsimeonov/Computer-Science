#### 7.2 Exercises

##### 1. Simplify each of the following

- (4)(3)(7)
- (7)(4)(3)
- (3)(4)(7)

Solution

- (4)(3)(7) = 84
  <br>
- (7)(4)(3) = 84
  <br>

- (3)(4)(7) = 84

---

##### 2. Simplify

- 5 x (4x2)
- (5x4) x 2

Solution

- 5 x (4x2) = 40
- (5x4) x 2 = 40

---

##### 3. Simplify each of the following

- 7(2z)
- 15(2y)
- (2)(3)x
- 9(3a)
- (11)(5a)
- 2(3x)

Solution

- 7(2z) = 14z
  <br>

- 15(2y) = 30y
  <br>

- (2)(3)x = 6x
  <br>

- 9(3a) = 27a
  <br>

- (11)(5a) = 55a
  <br>

- 2(3x) = 6x

---

##### 4. Simplify each of the following

- $5(4x^2)$
- $3(2y^3)$
- $11(2u^2)$
- $(2\times4)\times u^2$
- $(13)(2z^2)$

Solution

- $5(4x^2) = 20x^2$
  <br>

- $3(2y^3) = 6y^3$
  <br>

- $11(2u^2) = 22u^2$
  <br>

- $(2\times4)\times u^2 = 8u^2$
  <br>

- $(13)(2z^2) = 26z^2$

---
