##### 1. Write down the first five terms of the sequences given by:

(a) $x_k=3k$, starting from k = 1

(b) $x_k=4^k$, starting from k = 0

(c) $x_k=k/2$, starting from k = 1

(d) $x_k=7k-3$, starting from k = 0

(e) $x_k=-k$, starting form k = 0

Solution

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(a) $x_k=3k$, starting from k = 1</h6>

for k = 1
$x_k=3(1)=3$

for k = 2
$x_k=3(2)=6$

for k = 3
$x_k=3(3)=9$

for k = 4
$x_k=3(4)=12$

for k = 5
$x_k=3(5)=15$

The first five terms are 3,6,9,12,15

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(b) $x_k=4^k$, starting from k = 0</h6>

for k = 0
$x_k=4^{0}=1$

for k = 1
$x_k=4^{1}=4$

for k = 2
$x_k=4^{2}=16$

for k = 3
$x_k=4^{3}=64$

for k = 4
$x_k=4^{4}=256$

The first five terms are 1,4,16,64,256

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(c) $x_k=k/2$, starting from k = 1</h6>

for k = 1
$x_k=1/2=0.5$

for k = 2
$x_k=2/2=1$

for k = 3
$x_k=3/2=1.5$

for k = 4
$x_k=4/2=2$

for k = 5
$x_k=5/2=2.5$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(d) $x_k=7k-3$, starting from k = 0</h6>

for k = 0
$x_k=7(0)=-3$

for k = 1
$x_k=7(1)-3=4$

for k = 2
$x_k=7(2)-3=11$

for k = 3
$x_k=7(3)-3=18$

for k = 4
$x_k=7(4)-3=25$

<h6 style="color: #DCAE96">(e) $x_k=-k$, starting form k = 0</h6>

for k = 0
$x_k=0$

for k = 1
$x_k=-1$

for k = 2
$x_k=-2$

for k = 3
$x_k=-3$

for k = 4
$x_k=-4$

##### 1. Write down the first four terms of the sequence given by $x_n=2^n+3^n$, starting from n = 0

for n = 0
$x_n=2^0+3^0=1+1=2$

for n = 1
$x_n=2^1+3^1=5$

for n = 2
$x_n=2^2+3^2=4+9=13$

for n = 3
$x_n=2^3+3^3=8+27=35$
